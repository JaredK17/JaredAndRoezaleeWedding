OUR STORY

It all started with a hello.

Our story began long before we ever imagined it would lead us here. We first met in primary school and became friends throughout high school. Life eventually took us in different directions, and after losing touch for many years, our paths crossed again.

On 27 February 2021, we went on our first date — and the rest, as they say, is history. We even joked about having six-month dating contracts, never knowing just how far our little joke would take us.

For two years, Jared drove down every month just to spend time together, making every visit something special. In 2023, Roezalee moved to Johannesburg, bringing us closer and giving us the chance to create even more memories together.

From countless adventures and unforgettable moments to all the ordinary days that became extraordinary simply because we were together, our journey has brought us here.

And now, after all these years, we get to begin our greatest adventure yet forever together.

Every chapter of us began with a little adventure.

01

THE PROPOSAL

The moment everything changed.

31 July 2026 | we got engaged.

After many conversations about marriage throughout 2025, we somehow always found another excuse to postpone it. Then, in February 2026, what started as a joke quickly became a reality: "Why don't we just go and sign and tell everyone?"

So we drove down to KZN, made the decision, and set our wedding date. But, as life would have it, we soon realised that our firstborn, Jaden, would be writing his matric exams around that time, so once again, we went back to the drawing board.

Then came the thought that felt just right: how perfect would it be to get married on the day it all began? Our first date was on 27 February 2021, and now, five years later, we'll say "I do" on 27 February 2027.

What started as a joke, followed by years of conversations, plans, postponements and a spontaneous drive to KZN, has brought us to the moment we've been waiting for.

And on 31 July 2026, everything changed not because our story began that day, but because that was the day we knew for certain our forever was about to begin.
